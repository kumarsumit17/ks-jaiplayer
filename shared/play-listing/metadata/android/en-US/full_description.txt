KS JaiPlayer is a free, open-source player for the videos and music on your phone and your Google TV. It has no ads, no tracking and no account.

• Plays the video and audio files on your device, with subtitles and a choice of audio track.
• Watch a series episode by episode: open a folder and the next episode starts by itself, in the right order (episode 2 before episode 10).
• Playback speed from 0.5× to 2×.
• A Google TV app made for the remote: D-pad navigation, a 10-foot layout, and a built-in browser for your TV's videos and USB drives.
• Opens videos and stream links handed over by other apps, such as KS StreamFlix.
• Picture-in-picture on phones.
• English and Hindi, following your system language or your own choice.

KS JaiPlayer is part of KS-Suite, a set of free, private apps. The source code is on GitHub under the MIT licence: https://github.com/kumasumit/ks-jaiplayer
