KS JaiPlayer for Android and Google TV includes FFmpeg's AC-3 and E-AC-3 audio decoders.

FFmpeg 6.0.1 (https://ffmpeg.org), licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1
(COPYING.LGPLv2.1 beside this file). It is built as an LGPL library (no --enable-gpl, no
--enable-nonfree), with only the ac3 and eac3 decoders, libavutil and libswresample, and linked
into libffmpegJNI.so.

Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-6.0.1.tar.xz
(SHA-256 9b16b8731d78e596b4be0d720428ca42df642bb2d78342881ff7f5bc29fc9623), unmodified.
How it is built, with every configure option: shared/ffmpeg/build-ffmpeg.sh in
https://github.com/kumasumit/ks-jaiplayer. You may rebuild the library from that source and
replace libffmpegJNI.so in the app.

The JNI wrapper and the Kotlin classes around it come from AndroidX Media3's FFmpeg extension
(Apache License 2.0, Copyright The Android Open Source Project), changed for KS JaiPlayer.
