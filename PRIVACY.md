# KS JaiPlayer privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

KS JaiPlayer does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads.

## What stays on your device
- The files you play, your recent files, your settings (such as your language) and where you stopped. They never leave the device.
- On Google TV, the app asks for permission to read your videos and music only to list them in its own browser. It reads them on the device and sends nothing anywhere.

## When the app uses the network
- **Playing a link you open.** When you open an http or https stream link (for example from KS StreamFlix), the app connects to that address to play it.
- **Update check, only in the version from GitHub.** At most once a day the GitHub version asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) whether a newer version is out, and downloads it if you choose to update. You can turn this off in Settings. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that request. The version from Google Play has no update check; Google Play updates it.

Nothing else is sent.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, children included.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are published in this file, with a new date.
